3. Tester access

  • Tester spots are limited (currently 25) and offered by invitation. Signing in with LinkedIn does not guarantee a spot.
  • Tester access is free. Each tester has a daily and a monthly allowance on paid AI models (currently $1.00 a day and $10.00 a month), and all testers share a monthly budget. When an allowance is used up, requests stop until it resets. We may change these limits.
  • Read aloud with an AI voice counts against your allowance (typically about $0.02 per 1,000 characters; numbers, symbols, emoji and Chinese, Japanese or Korean text take longer to say and cost more per character) and is limited to about 20 requests a minute. When your allowance is used up, or a passage would take too long to say in one request, Read aloud uses your device’s own voice instead. The AI voice is AI-generated, not a human voice.
  • Dictation (the mic in the message box) uses Atelier’s transcription on an iPhone or iPad, or wherever your browser can’t transcribe speech itself. There it counts against your allowance (usually well under a cent per recording, though about $0.04 of it must be free to start one), recordings are up to 2 minutes long, and it is limited to about 20 recordings a minute. Elsewhere your browser’s own speech recognition is used, which doesn’t touch your allowance.
  • Look up (a short Wikipedia summary for words you select in an answer) is free and doesn’t touch your allowance. It starts on “On tap”, so nothing is sent until you tap Look up, and it is limited to about 30 look-ups a minute.
  • Testers cannot connect accounts (such as Google, Canva or Slack) or use browser control.
  • Private thread sync is off until you explicitly enable it. With your consent, threads, imported chats and media sync to private storage for your LinkedIn account, separately from the owner and other testers, with a 1 GiB cloud-storage limit (which also counts deleted threads’ saved copies and earlier versions) and up to 500 threads. Older threads in the previous shared browser database stay local unless you explicitly select and import them. Turning sync off stops transfers but keeps existing cloud copies; Clear this device removes only local data. Delete synced threads from the Threads drawer instead; they can be restored for 30 days. See the Privacy Policy for storage and deletion details.
  • We may pause, limit or end tester access at any time — for example, to protect the budget or if these terms are broken. Local threads remain on your device; synced copies follow the retention and deletion rules in the Privacy Policy.
  • Feedback is voluntary. Review the message, optional screenshot and basic diagnostics you choose to send; the studio owner can read them, and submissions are kept for up to 90 days as described in the Privacy Policy.
  • You are responsible for activity under your sign-in. Don’t share your session or try to get around the limits.
