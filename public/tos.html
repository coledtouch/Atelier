3. Tester access

  • Tester spots are limited (currently 25) and offered by invitation. Signing in with LinkedIn does not guarantee a spot.
  • Tester access is free. Each tester has a daily and a monthly allowance on paid AI models. Currently that is $1.00 a day and $10.00 a month. All testers share a monthly budget. When an allowance is used up, requests stop until it resets. We may change these limits.
  • Read aloud with an AI voice counts against your allowance. It typically costs about $0.02 per 1,000 characters. Numbers, symbols, emoji and Chinese, Japanese or Korean text take longer to say. They cost more per character. Read aloud is limited to about 20 requests a minute. If your allowance is used up, Read aloud uses your device’s own voice instead. It does the same when a passage would take too long to say in one request. Answers to spoken requests (Talk or the mic) are read aloud automatically. You can turn that off in Settings → Read aloud. Those readings count against your allowance the same way. The voice you hear is AI-generated.
  • Dictation is the mic in the message box. On an iPhone or iPad it uses Atelier’s transcription. It does the same wherever your browser can’t transcribe speech itself. There it counts against your allowance, usually well under a cent per recording. About $0.04 of your allowance must be free to start one. Recordings are up to 2 minutes long. Dictation is limited to about 20 recordings a minute. Elsewhere your browser’s own speech recognition is used. That doesn’t touch your allowance.
  • Look up shows a short Wikipedia summary for words you select in an answer. It is free and doesn’t touch your allowance. It starts on “On tap”, so nothing is sent until you tap Look up. It is limited to about 30 look-ups a minute.
  • Testers cannot connect accounts (such as Google, Canva or Slack) or use browser control.
  • Private thread sync is off until you explicitly enable it. With your consent, threads, imported chats and media sync to private storage for your LinkedIn account. That storage is kept separate from the owner and other testers. Your cloud storage is limited to 1 GiB and up to 500 threads. Deleted threads’ saved copies and earlier versions count toward the 1 GiB. Threads in the previous shared browser database stay local unless you explicitly select and import them. Turning sync off stops transfers but keeps existing cloud copies. Clear this device removes only local data. Delete synced threads from the Threads drawer instead. They can be restored for 30 days. See the Privacy Policy for storage and deletion details.
  • We may pause, limit or end tester access at any time. For example, we may do so to protect the budget or if these terms are broken. Local threads remain on your device. Synced copies follow the retention and deletion rules in the Privacy Policy.
  • Feedback is voluntary. Review the message, optional screenshot and basic diagnostics you choose to send. The studio owner can read them. Your submissions are kept for up to 90 days (see the Privacy Policy).
  • You are responsible for activity under your sign-in. Don’t share your session or try to get around the limits.
