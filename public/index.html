            Models

            Quick launch

            Atelier, one gesture away. Your phone keeps its own assistant button; these put Atelier right beside it.

            On your phone

            Android

            Install Atelier first: Chrome ⋮ → Add to Home screen → Install. Everything below needs the installed app.

            1. Talk button on your home screen. Touch and hold the Atelier icon, then drag Talk to Atelier onto your home screen. One tap opens Atelier listening.
            2. Back tap (Pixel 4a 5G and newer). Settings → System → Gestures → Quick tap → Use Quick tap → Open app → tap ⚙ → Atelier. If the list offers Talk to Atelier, choose it so a double-tap starts listening.
            3. Side button (Samsung). Settings → Advanced features → Side button → Double press → Apps → Atelier. If a ⚙ appears, choose Talk to Atelier.
            4. By voice. “Hey Google, open Atelier” (phone unlocked).
            5. Share sheet. Share a link, text, photo or video and choose Atelier.

            Holding the power button stays with your phone’s assistant — Android gives that only to native apps, like Atelier Assist below.

            After you speak (Talk to Atelier)

            Send goes about 1.5 seconds after you stop — tap Cancel or the prompt to hold it. Words added to an existing draft always wait for you.

            Start listening when I open Atelier

            Starts the mic when Atelier opens fresh from its icon, back tap, side button or “Hey Google”. It never sends without you. Signing out turns it off.

            Atelier Assist

            The Atelier Assist app on your side button sends what you say here, in the mode it picks. Pair it once: turn on Send without a tap, tap Copy Assist link, then in Atelier Assist tap ⋯ → Paste link.

            Send without a tap

            On adds a private key to the link that only this browser and the installed app accept. The first time, Atelier asks you to confirm. Video waits 4 seconds and shows its price before it starts. Signing out turns it off.

            When Chrome asks, choose “Allow while visiting the site” so it won’t ask again.

            iPhone

            iPhone keeps the side button for Siri, and a Shortcut can’t open the Home Screen app with your words. The Ask Atelier Shortcut listens, then opens Atelier in Safari with your words in the box.

            Set this up in Safari: open atelier.ciprari.ai in Safari, sign in there, then come back to Settings → Quick launch. Safari keeps its own sign-in.

            The link you copy here only fills the box, and you tap Send. For Send without a tap, copy the link on the iPhone itself, in Safari: its private key works only in the browser that copied it.

            1. On the iPhone, in Safari, sign in to Atelier.
            2. Send without a tap

              On adds a private key to your link that only this browser accepts. The first time, Atelier asks you to confirm. Signing out turns it off.

            3. Tap Copy Shortcut link.
              Get the Shortcut
            4. Tap Get the Shortcut and paste your link when it asks. Or build it yourself: Build the Shortcut: Shortcuts → + → name it Ask Atelier → add Dictate Text (Stop Listening: After Pause) → URL Encode (Dictated Text) → Text: paste your link, then insert URL Encoded Text right after q= → Open URLs (Text).
            5. Put it on a button:
              • Action button (iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 16 and later, 16e, 17e): Settings → Action Button → swipe to Shortcut → Choose a Shortcut → Ask Atelier.
              • Back Tap: Settings → Accessibility → Touch → Back Tap → Double Tap → Ask Atelier.
              • Control Center: open it → + → Add a Control → Shortcut → Ask Atelier.
              • Lock Screen: touch and hold the Lock Screen → Customize → Lock Screen → remove a bottom control → + → Shortcut → Ask Atelier.
              • Siri: “Hey Siri, Ask Atelier.”
            • Each run opens a new Safari tab.
            • If the phone is locked, iPhone asks you to unlock before Safari opens.
            • Safari can clear a site’s data after 7 days of using Safari without visiting it. If Ask Atelier lands on sign-in, sign in again and copy a fresh link.
            • Safari history (synced through iCloud) can keep the links Ask Atelier opens, including your words. New link retires the old key.
            • Don’t share a Shortcut that contains your link: with Send without a tap on, it carries your private key. Each person copies their own link in Settings → Quick launch.

            Your data

            Sync across your devices

            Threads sync privately through your Atelier server when you enable device sync. Owner access uses your passcode; LinkedIn testers use their own account. Older local threads are imported only when you select them. Settings, model choices and theme stay on each device.

            Export your threads and creations for safekeeping. Imports add copies without replacing existing work. Profile, account connections and preferences are not included.

            Your Claude chats. Bring your conversations in as threads you can open, continue and search. Files are read on this device without AI analysis.

            Where to find your Claude export

            On claude.ai, go to Settings → Privacy → Export data. The email links to several files: download conversations-000.zip (and any conversations-001.zip …) and choose it here, one at a time — not the manifest .json. Imported chats are stored in your account on this device and sync privately to your other devices when device sync is enabled. Importing a newer export adds only what’s new. The accounts agent can search imported chats when you ask it to.
